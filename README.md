**To Play:**
Simply press start and employ your curiosity into the plastic mind of the intellectual being.

Additional changes will include road-based interaction where you may introduce new and unexpected obstacles and challenges in the face of the intellectual body.

**Built:**

This game was built in Unity utilizing their advanced ML-Agents Reinforcement Learning Plugin on The Conda Terminal.

The Intellectual Body gets punished and rewarded based upon its accuracy of fulfilling steps. Thus overtime the intellect will reinforce its plasticity and thus cross the obstacle-riddled roads more accurately and efficiently.
